### [01:15 – 02:45] Why Does Satellite Work Take 3 to 6 Hours Today?
**On Screen:** *Show a messy computer screen with complicated software, big file folders, and someone drawing lines by hand.*

> "Have you ever wondered:
>
> *'Why does getting an answer from a satellite photo take 6 hours?'* `[pause]`
>
> Look at what an expert has to do today:
>
> **Step 1: The giant files.**  
> Satellite photos are huge. They are over 3 gigabytes each.  
> Just downloading and opening them on a computer takes 30 minutes.
>
> **Step 2: Crooked map lines.**  
> Satellites take photos from different angles in the sky.  
> When you put an old photo and a new photo together, they do not line up.  
> An expert has to spend an hour moving map points by hand so they match.
>
> **Step 3: The photos look pitch black.**  
> Real satellite cameras do not take bright phone photos.  
> The raw files look completely dark.  
> An expert has to adjust brightness, contrast, and clean up dots by hand.
>
> **Step 4: Drawing by hand.**  
> Finally, the expert sits with a computer mouse...  
> And slowly traces the water lines by hand, click by click.  
> Then they use a calculator to add up the damage.
>
> By the time all that slow work is done?
>
> **Three to six hours are gone.**
>
> When flood water is rising fast, waiting six hours is dangerous."

### [02:45 – 04:00] Why Can't We Just Use ChatGPT?
**On Screen:** *Show ChatGPT failing to open a satellite file. Show a simple comparison picture.*

> "Now, you might ask:
>
> *'Why not just upload the satellite photo to ChatGPT and ask it?'*
>
> That was the first thing we tested!
>
> But normal chatbots fail completely:
>
> **First: They throw away hidden light.**  
> Our eyes only see red, green, and blue.  
> But satellite cameras see invisible light, like infrared.  
> That hidden light is how we tell wet soil from dry land.  
> When you send a photo to ChatGPT, that hidden light is thrown away!
>
> **Second: They make up fake places.**  
> Normal chatbots do not know real GPS map numbers.  
> In fact, studies show chatbots guess wrong locations **34% of the time**!  
> In an emergency, you cannot send rescue boats to a fake location.
>
> **Third: They are blind to storm clouds.**  
> When a cyclone hits, normal cameras only see thick white clouds.  
> Chatbots look at the clouds and say: *'I only see white.'*  
> They cannot see the ground underneath.
>
> **Fourth: Data rules.**  
> You cannot send sensitive Indian satellite data to outside public servers."
